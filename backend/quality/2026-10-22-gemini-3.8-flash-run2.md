    - _Explanation: First, find the marked price: 200,000 + (25% of 200,000) = 250,000 UGX. Next, subtract the 10% discount: 250,000 - (10% of 250,000) = 250,000 - 25,000 = 225,000 UGX._

    - 4,200 UGX
    - 4,600 UGX
    - _Explanation: The new price represents 120% of the original price (100% + 20%). Divide 4,800 UGX by 120 to find 1%, which is 40 UGX, and multiply by 100 to get the original price of 4,000 UGX._

    - **Dear Babirye,** ✓

    - _Explanation: A friendly or informal letter uses an informal salutation with the person's first name._
3. (core, Grammar) Choose the sentence that means the same as:

    - Unless Mukasa weeded the maize garden, the crops would fail.
